Applied Physics

Notes

1) Introduction

  • Applied Physics is a branch of physics that uses the principles and laws of physics to solve practical problems in everyday life, science, and technology.

  • It connects theoretical physics with real-world applications such as electricity, electronics, mechanics, communication, medical equipment, computers, and engineering.

2) Importance

Applied Physics is important because it:

  • Supports engineering – Provides the basic knowledge needed for electrical, mechanical, civil, and other engineering fields.
  • Develops technology – Helps in the development of computers, electronics, communication systems, and modern machines.
  • Improves daily life – Physics is applied in appliances, transportation, electricity, medical devices, and many other areas.
  • Promotes innovation – Helps scientists and engineers create new materials, devices, and technologies.
  • Useful in industry – Improves manufacturing processes, energy systems, automation, and industrial equipment.

3) Types Of Applied Physics

The major types are:

  1. Applied Mechanics
    • Deals with motion, force, energy, and the behavior of physical objects. It is used in machines, vehicles, and structures.

  2. Applied Electronics
    • Deals with electronic components and circuits. It is used in computers, mobile phones, televisions, and control systems.

  3. Applied Thermodynamics
    • Deals with heat, temperature, energy, and their conversion. It is used in engines, refrigerators, and air-conditioning systems.
