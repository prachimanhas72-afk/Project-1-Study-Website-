Legal Method

Notes

1) Introduction

  • Legal Methods is a basic subject in legal education that teaches students how to understand, interpret, research, and apply the law. It helps students learn how to identify legal issues, find relevant laws and judicial decisions, understand legal principles, and apply those principles to particular facts.

  • Legal Methods introduces students to the major sources of law, such as the Constitution, statutes enacted by the legislature, judicial precedents, customs, and delegated legislation. It also explains how courts interpret and apply these sources while deciding cases

2) Importance

Legal Method is important because it:

  • Develops Legal Reasoning: It helps students understand and apply legal principles logically.
  • Improves Legal Research: It teaches how to find and use statutes, case laws, legal articles, and other sources of law.
  • Helps in Case Analysis: It enables students to identify the facts, legal issues, arguments, and decisions in a case.
  • Improves Legal Writing: It develops the ability to present legal arguments and conclusions clearly and systematically.
  • Useful in Legal Practice: It provides practical skills needed by lawyers, judges, legal researchers, and law students to solve legal problems effectively.

3) Types Of Legal Method

The major types are:

  1. Doctrinal Legal Method
    • It studies statutes, case laws, legal principles, and rules to understand the existing law.
    • It focuses on finding and interpreting what the law is.

  2. Comparative Legal Method
    • It compares the laws of different countries or legal systems.
    • It helps identify similarities, differences, and better legal practices.

  3. Historical Legal Method
    • It studies the origin and development of laws and legal institutions.
    • It helps understand how present laws have evolved from the past.
