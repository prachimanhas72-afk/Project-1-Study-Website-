Architectural Graphic Skills

Notes

1) Introduction

  • Architectural Graphic Skills are the techniques used by architects and students to represent and communicate architectural ideas through drawings and visual presentations. These skills include freehand sketching, technical drawing, lettering, geometric construction, perspective drawing, rendering, plans, elevations, and sections.

  • Architectural graphics help to clearly show the shape, size, proportion, materials, spaces, and appearance of a building. They are essential for developing design ideas and communicating them effectively to clients, engineers, contractors, and other professionals.

2) Importance

Architectural graphic skills are important because it:

  • Makes it easier to understand the shape, form, spaces, and appearance of a building.
  • Helps explain architectural ideas through drawings and sketches.
  • Good graphics make architectural projects clear and attractive during presentations.
  • Drawings such as plans, elevations, and sections provide important information for construction.
  • Graphic skills are essential for architects and help them communicate effectively with clients and other professionals.

3) Types Of Architectural Graphic Skills

The major types are:

  1. Perspective Drawing
    • Includes tiles, paint, plaster, flooring, and laminates, which improve the appearance and protection of surfaces.

  2. Architectural Plans
    • A top view of a building showing the arrangement of rooms, walls, doors, windows, and circulation.

  3. Computer-Aided Drawing (CAD)
    • Uses computer software to create accurate 2D drawings and 3D models efficiently
