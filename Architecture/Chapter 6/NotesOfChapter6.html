Problem Solving and Creative Thinking

Notes

1) Introduction

  • Problem solving focuses on identifying a problem, analysing it, and finding a suitable solution, while creative thinking involves generating new and original ideas.

  • In architecture and design, these skills help students and professionals explore different possibilities and make better design decisions. They encourage imagination, observation, critical thinking, experimentation, and innovation, which are necessary for creating functional, practical, and aesthetically pleasing designs.

2) Importance

Architectural graphic skills are important because it:

  • Combines creativity with practical thinking to create designs that are both functional and attractive.
  • Helps solve problems related to space, structure, materials, climate, cost, and user needs.
  • Gives the ability to face difficult situations and find solutions independently.
  • Helps compare different options and select the best ones and encourages analysing situations carefully before making decisions.

3) Types Of Architectural Graphic Skills

The major types are:

  1. Analytical Problem Solving
    • Involves studying the problem carefully, finding its causes, and developing a logical solution.

  2. Critical Thinking
    • Examining and evaluating ideas to determine their usefulness and practicality.

  3. Decision-Based Problem Solving
    • Compares different alternatives and selects the most appropriate solution.
