Basic Design And Visual Arts

Notes

1) Introduction

  • Basic design deals with the principles and elements used to create visually balanced and meaningful compositions, while visual arts focus on expressing ideas and emotions through drawing, painting, colour, form, texture, and other visual elements.

  • The subject helps students develop creativity, observation, imagination, and visual communication skills. It also provides a foundation for understanding line, shape, form, colour, space, texture, balance, rhythm, proportion, and harmony, which are essential for architectural and artistic design.

2) Importance

Basic Design and Visual Arts are important because it:

  • Helps express ideas and concepts through drawings, colours, forms, and compositions.
  • Provides a foundation for creating building forms, spaces, patterns, and presentations.
  • Regular practice in drawing and creative activities improves artistic and presentation skills.
  • Teaches balance, proportion, rhythm, harmony, contrast, and unity.

3) Types Of Basic Design and Visual Arts

The major types are:

  1. Colour Study
    • Understanding primary, secondary, warm, cool, and complementary colours and their combinations.

  2. Form and Shape Study
    • Exploring 2D shapes and 3D forms to understand their visual and spatial qualities.

  3. Printmaking
    • Creating images by transferring designs from a prepared surface onto paper or another material.
